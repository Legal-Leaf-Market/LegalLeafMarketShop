# THE GRAND RAPIDS TRAP IS NOT DETROIT'S, AND IT IS EASIER TO MISS.
#
# There is no enclave here -- no Hamtramck sitting inside the city. What there
# is instead is a RING OF SEPARATE MUNICIPALITIES that a directory, a mailing
# address and a shop's own marketing all call "Grand Rapids": WYOMING,
# KENTWOOD, WALKER, GRANDVILLE, COMSTOCK PARK, EAST GRAND RAPIDS and GRAND
# RAPIDS TOWNSHIP. Each has its own council, its own opt-in decision and its own
# line in the Treasury distribution, so none of their shops is one of the 27.
#
# Comstock Park and Grand Rapids Township are the two that catch people, because
# both carry "Grand Rapids" in the postal address. An Alpine Avenue shop may be
# in the city or in Walker depending on which side of the line it sits.
#
# The sweep's cityOnPage guard does NOT save you here, because a Wyoming shop's
# page says "Grand Rapids" all over it -- that guard was built for a chain's
# national site, not for a neighbour three miles away. Check the street address
# against the city limits before promoting anything on this list.
#
# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
