## Specs

### 1. Sticker sheet
- Kiss-cut sheet, about 5.5 x 8.5 in, matte or holographic vinyl, waterproof.
- On it: the three strain medallions, the gold G.O.B. logo, the round badge.

### 2. Medallion patch
- 3 in round, **printed (sublimated) or high-density woven**, not plain embroidery.
  The stained-glass medallions are too detailed for stitching and would lose the glass
  effect. Merrowed border in copper thread.
- Iron-on or hook-and-loop backing: ask for both prices.
- One design per patch. Quote a mix of the three strains, about a third each. Ask whether
  each design carries its own setup fee or minimum.

### 3. Pin set
- Three pins, 1.25 in, one per strain medallion, on a printed backing card with the G.O.B.
  logo.
- The art is painterly, so ask for **printed pins with an epoxy dome** (offset print on
  metal) rather than soft enamel. Soft enamel would need the art redrawn as flat color
  fields. Ask the vendor which they recommend.
- Antique copper or gold metal plating, two rubber clutches each.
- Ask whether any minimum applies per design or per order.

### 4. Hat
- Unstructured dad hat or a 6-panel snapback, forest green, copper embroidery.
- Front: the stacked G.O.B. logo. **It will need a vector or digitized file**: we only have
  PNGs. Ask whether digitizing is included.

### 5. Tee
- Heavyweight cotton, forest green or black.
- Front: small gold logo on the chest, one-color copper or cream screen print.
- Back: the three medallions, full color. **One-color screen print cannot carry this art.**
  Ask the vendor to price DTF, DTG or simulated process, and to say which holds the detail
  best on a dark shirt.
- Sizes S to 3XL. Quote a size curve of S 10%, M 25%, L 30%, XL 20%, 2XL 10%, 3XL 5%, and
  state any 2XL and 3XL upcharge. Round the curve to whole shirts. Place the final order
  from the sizes members actually pick.

### 6. Hoodie (Summit only)
- Heavyweight pullover, forest green, same print layout and method as the tee.
- Same size curve and upcharge question as the tee.
- Quote at 25 and 50. Summit is capped at 50, so a 100-unit price would never apply.

### 7. Numbered brass Founder coin (Summit only)
- 1.75 in, die-struck brass, antique finish, about 3 mm thick.
- Front: the round G.O.B. badge. Back: "FOUNDER" plus the **individual number 1 to 50**
  (laser engraved or stamped), with a blank for each spare.
- Soft PVC sleeve or a small velvet pouch.
- Die striking is relief, not print, so the badge art must be simplified for the die. Ask
  whether that art and the mold fee are included, and for a proof of the struck version.

### 8. Mailers
- **Trailhead and Lodge:** padded envelope. Sticker sheet and patch only.
- **Ridge:** small box. It has to hold a hat or a folded tee, plus pins.
- **Summit:** a larger box. It has to hold a hoodie, a hat or tee, pins, and a coin. Test
  the fit with a real hoodie before ordering boxes.
- A one-color logo is a nice-to-have, plain is fine.

### Color
Hex values do not travel to apparel. Ask each apparel vendor for the closest stock blank to
dark forest green (reference #0f2a1d), and pick from a swatch or sample. Ask for the closest
copper thread and ink match by thread number or Pantone.

### Art files
Send vendors the full-size originals from the Golden Hemp package as PNG or PDF, not the
website `.webp` files. Ask each vendor what format and resolution they want.

## How to read the quotes

Use the tracker (`GOB_merch_quote_tracker.xlsx`). Count **landed unit cost**: unit price,
plus setup, mold, art, digitizing and freight divided by the quantity. Setup fees are a real
share of a 50-unit order.

For each tier, add up its items, plus its mailer and postage:

- **Use the 100-unit landed cost** for stickers, patches, pins, hats, tees, envelopes and
  small boxes.
- **Use the 50-unit landed cost for the hoodie and Summit box**, and the **55-coin batch cost
  divided by 50** for the coin, because Summit cannot go past 50.
- **For hat or tee, use whichever costs more,** since the member picks.

If a tier's total is more than **half the tier price**, the tier loses money once card fees,
misprints and the launch event are paid. In that case drop an item or raise the price before
launch, not after members have paid.

| Tier | Ceiling | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Trailhead | $12.50 | half of $25 |
| Ridge | $37.50 | half of $75 |
| Summit | $125.00 | half of $250 |
| Lodge | $15.00 per drop | half of the $30 paid per quarter |

**Then check the slow case.** The 100-unit price assumes the club fills. The coin batch, and
any setup fees, cost the same however few people join, so check Summit at a low member count
in the tracker's scenario tab before you commit.

**Refunds.** The announcement promises a full refund any time before a pack ships. Merch you
have already bought stays bought, so size the first order to paid members, not to hopes.

## Things to ask every vendor
- Unit price at each quantity, setup or mold fees, and art or digitizing fees.
- Proof process (digital and physical sample) and the cost of a sample.
- Production time and shipping time to Columbus, Indiana.
- **Whether they will print branding that says "Cannabis."** Some vendors refuse it, and
  it is better to know before the proof stage. The merch itself carries no product claims.
