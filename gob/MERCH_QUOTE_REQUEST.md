Everything the Founders Club tiers promise, specced so a vendor can quote it. Send the
email at the bottom to two or three vendors per item, compare, then set the tier prices.
Nothing here commits to an order.

## Specs

### 1. Sticker sheet
- Kiss-cut sheet, about 5.5 x 8.5 in, matte or holographic vinyl, waterproof.
- On it: the three strain medallions, the gold G.O.B. logo, the round badge.
- Art: `public/img/gob/*-medallion.webp`, `logo-gold.png`, `founders-badge.jpg` (full-size
  originals in the Golden Hemp package).

### 2. Medallion patch
- 3 in round, **printed (sublimated) or high-density woven**, not plain embroidery.
  The stained-glass medallions are too detailed for stitching and would lose the glass
  effect. Merrowed border in copper thread.
- Iron-on or hook-and-loop backing: ask for both prices.
- One design per patch. Quote a mix of the three strains, about a third each.

### 3. Enamel pin set
- Three pins, 1.25 in, one per strain medallion, on a printed backing card with the G.O.B.
  logo.
- The art is painterly, so ask for **printed pins with an epoxy dome** (offset print on
  metal) rather than soft enamel. Soft enamel would need the art redrawn as flat color
  fields. Ask the vendor which they recommend.
- Antique copper or gold metal plating, two rubber clutches each.

### 4. Hat
- Unstructured dad hat or a 6-panel snapback, forest green (close to #0f2a1d), copper
  embroidery.
- Front: the stacked G.O.B. logo. **It will need a vector or digitized file**: we only have
  PNGs. Ask whether digitizing is included.

### 5. Tee
- Heavyweight cotton, forest green or black, one-color copper or cream screen print.
- Front: small gold logo on the chest. Back: the three medallions, as a full-color print.
- Sizes S to 3XL. Quote a size curve of S 10%, M 25%, L 30%, XL 20%, 2XL 10%, 3XL 5%.

### 6. Hoodie (Summit only)
- Heavyweight pullover, forest green, same print layout as the tee.
- Quote at 50 and 100 only. Summit is capped at 50.

### 7. Numbered brass Founder coin (Summit only)
- 1.75 in, die-struck brass, antique finish, about 3 mm thick.
- Front: the round G.O.B. badge. Back: "FOUNDER" plus the **individual number 1 to 50**
  (laser engraved or stamped), with a blank for each spare.
- Soft PVC sleeve or a small velvet pouch.

### 8. Mailer
- Rigid flat mailer or small box for the Ridge and Summit packs, padded envelope for
  Trailhead. A one-color logo is a nice-to-have, plain is fine.

## How to read the quotes

Add up each tier's items at the 100-unit price, plus its mailer and postage. If that total
is more than **half the tier price**, the tier loses money once card fees, misprints and
the launch event are paid. In that case drop an item or raise the price before launch,
not after members have paid. Ceilings: Trailhead $12.50, Ridge $37.50, Summit $125.

## Things to ask every vendor
- Unit price at each quantity, setup or mold fees, and art or digitizing fees.
- Proof process (digital and physical sample) and the cost of a sample.
- Production time and shipping time to Columbus, Indiana.
- **Whether they will print branding that says "Cannabis."** Some vendors refuse it, and
  it is better to know before the proof stage. The merch itself carries no product claims.

## Where to start
For example: Sticker Mule (stickers), GS-JJ or Vivipins (pins, patches, coins), a local
Columbus screen printer for hats, tees and hoodies. A print-on-demand service such as
Printful would avoid buying apparel stock up front, at a higher unit cost. Check its
content rules first.
