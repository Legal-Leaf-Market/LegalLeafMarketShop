# Brief for ChatGPT: G.O.B. creative production

**Today is 5 Nov 2026.** G.O.B. / Good Old Boys is a new brand from Columbus, Indiana,
launching three 2 mL disposable vape flavors (Strawberry Pie, Lemon Cherry Gelato, Purple
Haze) and a paid **Founders Club**: merch packs and perks for early backers. Research and
legal questions are going to a different assistant. **Your job is making things:** art
variants and copy.

Attach these images from the repo (`Legal-Leaf-Market/LegalLeafMarketShop`) when you start:
`public/img/gob/logo-gold.png`, `founders-badge.jpg`, and the three
`*-medallion.webp` files.

## The brand, so everything matches

- **Colors:** deep forest green (#0f2a1d), aged copper (#c9853f), light copper (#e2a866),
  warm cream (#efe6cf).
- **Look:** stained glass, gears, a compass rose, hammered copper rims. Old-world workshop,
  not neon, not cartoon.
- **Voice:** plain, warm, a little Midwestern. "Good old boys," not corporate.

## Rules (all tasks)

1. **No em dashes** anywhere. Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses.
2. **No effect claims:** nothing like relaxing, uplifting, energizing, calming or "feel".
   Talk about flavor, craft and the club.
3. **No cannabis leaves, smoke, vapor clouds or people using a product** in any image.
4. **No THC numbers or strain lineage.**
5. **Founders Club copy always says** it is merch and perks, **not an investment**, does not
   buy a share of the company, and is **21+**.
6. **Never invent facts:** no dates, member counts, awards or reviews. Use `[brackets]` for
   anything we have to fill in.

## Tasks, most useful first

### 1. Simplified art for physical merch
The medallions are painterly and lose detail when stitched, struck or enameled. Make:
1. **A one-color version of the G.O.B. stacked logo** (mountain, "G.O.B.", "GOOD OLD BOYS"),
   flat shapes, no gradients, thick enough to embroider at 2.5 in wide on a hat front.
2. **A relief version of the round badge for a brass coin die**: two levels only (raised and
   recessed), no color, no fine lines under about 0.3 mm at 1.75 in across. Make the front
   (badge) and the back (the word "FOUNDER", a ring, and an empty space for a number).
3. **Flat-color pin versions of the three medallions** with no more than 8 colors each, keeping
   the gear rim and compass. Bold black outlines between color areas, as a pin needs.

Deliver each as a high-resolution PNG on a transparent or white background. If you can
export SVG, do.

### 2. Tee back print
A back print for a forest-green or black tee: the three medallions in a row with "G.O.B.
FOUNDERS CLUB" and "COLUMBUS, INDIANA" beneath. Print area about 12 x 14 in. Two options:
one full color, one limited to 4 spot colors.

### 3. Fix three of the existing mockup scenes
Recreate these as cleaner versions:
1. **Purple Haze staged scene:** the same box and device layout, props only lavender
   sprigs, blueberries and blackberries. No citrus peel.
2. **Lemon Cherry Gelato box:** the vertical "G.O.B." on the side spine reads mirrored in the
   current render. Redo it reading correctly, top to bottom.
3. **One social-safe hero image:** the three devices standing on dark stone with copper gears
   and the flavor ingredients (strawberries, lemon, cherries, lavender) only. No leaves, no
   smoke.

### 4. Founders Club page FAQ
Write 8 to 10 questions and answers for the club page. Must cover: what each tier gets, when
packs ship (`[ship window]`), refunds (full refund any time before your pack ships), what
"vote on the next flavor" means and how it happens, how the Founders Wall works (opt-in,
your name as you typed it), Lodge billing and cancelling, 21+, and that it is not an
investment. Keep each answer to 2 or 3 sentences.

### 5. Packing insert card
A 4 x 6 in card that goes in every Founders pack: a thank-you from Jacob and `[partner name]`,
the member's tier, how to vote, and a line to tag `@[instagram handle]`. Front and back copy,
plus a layout sketch.

### 6. Welcome email sequence
Three short emails for new members:
1. **Welcome**, sent right after joining: what they get and what happens next.
2. **Your pack is on the way**, with a `[tracking link]` placeholder.
3. **Time to vote**, inviting them to vote on the fourth flavor, with a `[vote link]` placeholder.

Each email gets a subject line, preview text and a body under 150 words.

### 7. Ten social captions
Ten Instagram captions for the first month of the club. Mix them: tier spotlights, the coin,
the medallion art, behind the scenes, the Founders Wall, a vote reminder. Same rules as above.
No selling vapes in any caption, only the club and merch. 2 to 4 sentences each, up to 5
hashtags.

## How to hand it back

For copy, one Markdown section per task with the task number as the heading. For images,
name each file like `gob-<task>-<short-name>.png` (for example `gob-1-coin-front.png`) so
they can go straight into the repo.
