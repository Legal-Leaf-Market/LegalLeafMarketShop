# Research brief for Gemini, round 2: G.O.B. launch

**Today is 5 Nov 2026.** Same project as round 1: G.O.B. / Good Old Boys, Columbus,
Indiana. Three 2 mL hemp-derived THC disposables (Strawberry Pie, Lemon Cherry Gelato,
Purple Haze) from a white-label supplier, plus a paid Founders Club of merch and perks
sold on Shopify (shop.legalleafmarket.shop).

**Decisions already made, so do not argue them:** the vapes sell **in person only, in
Columbus, before 11 Dec 2026**. They will never be sold online or shipped. The flavor names
stay as they are. Round 1 already covered the federal timeline, carriers, Shopify Payments
and THC, social media rules and the securities question, so do not repeat those.

## Rules for every answer (same as round 1)

- **Cite a source for every claim**, with the URL and its published or updated date.
  Primary sources (statute text, agency pages, a company's own policy) first.
- **Say how current it is.** Flag anything older than six months.
- **Never guess.** Write `UNCONFIRMED` and what would confirm it.
- **Not legal advice.** Flag anything a lawyer should confirm.
- Use the reply format at the bottom.

## 1. Selling in person in Indiana before 11 Dec (most urgent)

1. **Indiana's labeling law for hemp products.** Indiana regulates "low THC hemp extract"
   labels (believed to be IC 24-4-21; confirm the citation). List every element a label must
   carry: QR code to the lab report, batch number, expiration date, manufacturer, ingredient
   list, THC statement, or anything else. Does it apply to a vape? Our device wrap currently
   shows 21+, keep out of reach, net 2 mL, a batch and best-by line, and a QR code. **What is
   missing?**
2. **Does Indiana require registration** to sell hemp products at retail? For example, a
   distributor or retailer registration with the Office of Indiana State Chemist, a retail
   merchant certificate, or product registration. Give the agency, the form, the fee and
   how long it takes.
3. **"Smoke" versus vapor.** IC 35-48-1-26.6 defines smokable hemp as a form that delivers
   THC "by inhalation of smoke." Find any Indiana court decision, Attorney General opinion,
   Indiana State Police or prosecutor statement, or enforcement action that says whether a
   vape counts. Name the case or document.
4. **Columbus and Bartholomew County.** Any local ordinance, license or zoning rule on selling
   hemp THC, vapes or smoke products in person.
5. **What happens to unsold stock on 11 Dec?** Any federal guidance (DEA, FDA, USDA) on a
   sell-through period, safe harbor or required disposal for products that stop being hemp
   on that date.

## 2. Finding the right supplier

Round 1 could not tie "Golden Hemp Co." to one company.

1. Search for a **white-label supplier called Golden Hemp Co. offering 2 mL iKrusher
   disposables with custom wraps**. List every candidate with website, location and any
   business registration you can find.
2. List **three to five other white-label suppliers** of 2 mL hemp THC disposables on
   iKrusher hardware that publish full-panel lab reports (potency, heavy metals, pesticides,
   residual solvents) from an ISO 17025 lab. Include minimum order and lead time where
   published.
3. **Lead time reality check:** for a custom-wrap run, what turnaround do these suppliers
   state from artwork approval to delivery? Can a run ordered this week realistically be in
   hand and sold before 11 Dec?

## 3. Running the Founders Club on Shopify

1. **Shopify Subscriptions app:** which payment gateways does it support? Can a monthly
   membership (our $10 Lodge tier) run on Shopify Payments, and what are the alternatives if
   Shopify Payments is not available?
2. **Stripe's restricted businesses list** (Shopify Payments runs on Stripe): does selling
   merch and memberships under a brand name containing "Cannabis" fall under it? Quote the
   relevant line. Find any reported cases of a merch-only cannabis-branded store being shut
   off.
3. **Indiana sales tax setup:** how a new seller registers (INTIME, the Retail Merchant
   Certificate), the fee, and how to itemize a membership in Shopify so tax applies only to
   the merch portion, if itemizing is allowed.
4. **Running a member vote** (our members vote on the fourth flavor): free or cheap tools that
   limit one vote per paying member and work with Shopify customer accounts.
