# Research brief for Gemini: G.O.B. launch

**Today is 5 Nov 2026.** G.O.B. / Good Old Boys is a new brand from Columbus, Indiana. It
is about to place a first white-label order of 2 mL hemp-derived THC disposable vapes
(iCrusher hardware) from **Golden Hemp Co.**, in three flavors: Strawberry Pie, Lemon Cherry
Gelato and Purple Haze. It plans to sell them locally in person and online through a
Shopify store (shop.legalleafmarket.shop, operated by Legal-Leaf Market). It is also
launching a paid **Founders Club**: merch packs and perks, with no ownership or returns.

We need facts before money moves. Work the tasks **in order**: task 1 can make the rest
moot.

## Rules for every answer

- **Cite a source for every claim**, with the URL and the date the source was published or
  last updated. Primary sources (statute text, agency guidance, a company's own policy page)
  beat news articles, and news articles beat blogs.
- **Say how current it is.** Laws and platform policies here changed in 2025 and 2026. If the
  newest source you can find is more than six months old, say so.
- **Never guess.** If you cannot confirm something, write `UNCONFIRMED` and say what would
  confirm it. A wrong "yes" costs more than an honest "unknown."
- **Not legal advice.** Report what the sources say. Flag anything a lawyer should confirm.
- Use the reply format at the bottom for each task.

## 1. Is the product legal to sell, federally and in Indiana? (most urgent)

1. The **November 2025 federal appropriations law** redefined "hemp." What is the exact
   **effective date**, and what does the new definition do to a hemp-derived THC vape? Is
   there a per-container THC limit (reported as 0.4 mg), and does it count THCA, delta-8 and
   other intoxicating cannabinoids?
2. After that date, is a 2 mL hemp-derived THC disposable legal to **sell** anywhere in the
   US, or only where a state has its own cannabis program?
3. **Indiana:** what does current Indiana law say about hemp-derived THC vapes (delta-9,
   delta-8, THCA)? Indiana bans "smokable hemp": does that ban cover vape cartridges or
   disposables? Name the statute section (IC citation).
4. Any **2026 Indiana bills** on hemp-derived THC: passed, pending or failed, with status.
5. What has happened since the federal change took effect, or is about to? For example,
   enforcement actions, court challenges, or an extension of the effective date.

## 2. Golden Hemp Co. due diligence

1. Who is Golden Hemp Co. (or the company behind it)? Location, business registration,
   how long trading, and its website.
2. Reviews, complaints, lawsuits or regulatory actions involving it.
3. What it says about its **lab testing (COAs)**: which labs, ISO 17025 accreditation, and
   whether it tests each batch for potency, heavy metals, pesticides and residual solvents.
4. Its white-label terms, where public: minimum order, unit price ranges, lead times.
5. **iCrusher hardware:** who makes it, and any known recalls, safety complaints or heavy
   metal (lead) testing issues with that model.

## 3. Shipping vapes to customers

1. Does the **PACT Act / USPS mailing ban** on ENDS cover hemp-derived THC vapes? Cite the
   USPS rule (Publication 52) and any guidance on hemp vapes.
2. Current consumer-shipping policies at **UPS, FedEx and DHL** for vapes and for
   hemp-derived THC products. Quote the policy text.
3. What do hemp vape brands that ship legally actually use? Name carriers or services, and
   any requirements such as adult signature or state registration.

## 4. Payments and the Shopify store

1. **Shopify Payments acceptable use policy:** is a **merch-only membership** sold by a brand
   with "Cannabis" in its name allowed? Are hemp-derived THC products allowed? Quote the
   policy.
2. Can a Shopify store use a **third-party payment processor** for hemp-derived THC products?
   List processors that currently accept hemp THC vapes and work with Shopify, with fees and
   approval requirements.
3. **Age verification** apps for Shopify that check ID for 21+ at checkout: names, prices,
   and whether they meet state requirements.

## 5. Promoting it on social media

1. **Meta (Instagram and Facebook)** current policy on cannabis and hemp brands: can a brand
   account post about merch and a members' club if it never sells THC in posts? What gets
   accounts restricted?
2. Same question for **TikTok**.

## 6. Trademarks

1. Search **USPTO** (and note any state marks you can find) for "G.O.B.", "GOB" and
   "Good Old Boys" in classes 34 (vapes), 25 (apparel) and 35 (retail). List any live marks
   that could conflict.
2. **"Purple Haze"**: is it a registered trademark (for example, connected to the Jimi
   Hendrix estate) in a class that covers vapes or related goods? Any enforcement against
   cannabis brands using it?
3. Same check for **"Strawberry Pie"** and **"Lemon Cherry Gelato"** as product names.

## 7. Founders Club structure

1. Confirm that a membership sold for **merch and perks only** (no ownership, no profit share,
   no returns) is not a security under federal law or Indiana's securities act. Cite the
   test used (Howey) and any Indiana guidance.
2. If they later want a **real co-op** that members partly own: what does Indiana law
   require, and do any Reg CF crowdfunding portals currently accept hemp or cannabis
   businesses?
3. **Indiana sales tax** on a membership that includes physical merch: is it taxed, and on
   what amount?

## 8. Merch vendors

For each of these vendors, find their content policy on **cannabis branding**, and price
benchmarks for our specs: Stomp Stickers, Sticker Mule, Signature Patches, Vivipins,
GS-JJ, Lapel Pins Plus, Signature Pins, Signature Coins, Packlane, noissue, eyb promotions,

Specs to price: a 5.5 x 8.5 in kiss-cut sticker sheet; a 3 in sublimated or woven patch;
a set of three 1.25 in printed pins with epoxy; a 1.75 in die-struck brass coin at 55 units,
individually numbered; an embroidered dad hat; a heavyweight tee with a full-color back print
(DTF or DTG).

## 9. Market pricing

Retail prices for **2 mL hemp-derived THC disposables** sold online and in Indiana shops in
2026: list at least 10 products with brand, price, store and date seen. What is the typical
range?
