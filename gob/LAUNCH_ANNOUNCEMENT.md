## Rules this copy follows, so keep them when you edit

- **It sells the club, not the vapes.** Instagram and Facebook take down posts that sell
  THC, and ad accounts get banned for it. The club is merch and perks, so it can be
  promoted. No device prices, no "buy," no cannabis leaves or smoke in the images.
- **No effect claims** ("relaxing," "uplifting") and no THC numbers.
- **"Not an investment" stays in** anywhere tiers or money are mentioned. Members get
  perks, not a share.
- **21+** on every version.
- **Real scarcity only.** Summit is 50 because the store stops at 50. Do not add "only a
  few left" unless the page says so.
