## Checked against independent sources (5 Nov 2026)

- **The federal hemp definition change is real and dated.** The November 2025
  appropriations law redefined hemp. A continuing resolution (H.R. 6500, P.L. 119-103,
  signed 2 Sep 2026) moved most of it from 12 Nov to **11 Dec 2026**. From then, a product
  with more than **0.4 mg total THC per container** is not hemp federally. Cannabinoids the
  plant cannot naturally produce lose hemp status on **12 Nov 2026**. The delay lasts only
  as long as that continuing resolution, so it could be extended in December, or not.
  Sources: The Hill, CRS (congress.gov IF13136), CSP Daily News, Vicente LLP, Foley Hoag.
- **A 2 mL THC disposable is far over 0.4 mg**, so after 11 Dec it is not federal hemp.

## Gemini's findings not yet checked independently (treat as leads)

- Indiana's smokable-hemp ban (IC 35-48-1-26.6) may reach vapes. The statute's wording is
  "smoke", and vapor is arguable, so this needs a lawyer.
- 2026 Indiana bill SB 250 passed a House committee and did not become law.
- USPS (PACT Act) and UPS, FedEx and DHL all refuse hemp THC vapes to consumers. This
  matches what `CLAUDE.md` already records.
- "Golden Hemp Co." could not be tied to one legal entity. Get their W-9, certificate of
  good standing and hemp license number before paying anything.
- Shopify Payments does not support hemp, CBD or THC products.
- The Founders Club as merch and perks only is not a security under the Howey test, but a
  lawyer should read the final terms.
- Indiana sales tax (7%) likely applies to the whole membership price when it is one
  bundled price including merch.
- "Purple Haze" is a trademark claimed by the Jimi Hendrix estate (Experience Hendrix LLC),
  with a history of enforcement against cannabis goods.
