Pull up a chair

Part cafe, part bodega, part living room.

We're opening a real neighborhood spot with a real counter and a real kettle. Grab a good cup of coffee or loose-leaf tea, pick up something from the shelf, and stay awhile. It's handmade, a little bohemian, and the kind of place you can walk into without being sold anything.
