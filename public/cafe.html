Pull up a chair

Part cafe, part bodega, part living room.

We're opening a real neighborhood spot with a real counter and a real kettle. Grab a good cup of coffee or loose-leaf tea, pick up something from the shelf, and stay awhile. It's handmade, a little bohemian, and the kind of place you can walk into without being sold anything.

Step inside

Small menu, done properly.

The brew bar

Espresso, Americanos and lattes pulled to order, plus drip coffee and whole beans to take home.

Loose-leaf tea

Steeped right in the cup you walk out with, including our lotus and mullein herbal tea with a soft, earthy finish.

The herb shelf

Loose herbs by the jar, like mullein and lavender, plus tea and herb blends from makers we trust.

The sound of the place

A small stage with a rug under it.

Live acoustic music nights are on the way: a stool, a quiet room, solo players and duos. It's the kind of set you can talk over if you want to, and listen to if you don't.
