Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026

The short version. Browsing is anonymous. When you order, we collect what we need to confirm your age and deliver your order, and nothing else. We never sell your information.

What we collect when you order

WhatWhy
Name, date of birthTo confirm you are 21 or older, and to match the photo ID we check at delivery.
Delivery addressTo deliver your order and to check it is in our Indiana delivery area.
Phone and emailTo confirm your order, agree a delivery time, and answer questions.
What you orderedTo fill the order, handle returns, and keep the sales records Indiana tax law requires.

We look at your ID at the door; we do not photograph, scan or keep a copy of it.

Payments

You pay in cash at delivery. We never ask for card or bank details, online or at your door.

What stays in your browser

Your cart and your answer to the 21+ question are saved in your own browser's local storage, so they are there when you come back. They never leave your device until you place an order. This shop sets no advertising cookies and runs no ad trackers.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to complete your order: the email and hosting services that run this site and deliver your order details to us. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as Indiana and federal tax rules require (generally several years). Anything not needed for that, we delete when it is no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete anything we are not required to keep. Email jake@legalleafmarket.shop.

Children

This site and everything on it is for adults 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.
