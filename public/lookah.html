Lookah: Seahorse Dab Pens, Glass Bongs & Dab Rigs

Lookah makes its own hardware — electric nectar collectors, 510 batteries, e‑rigs and enails — alongside a glass line of bongs and dab rigs. Everything below is their catalogue, at their prices.

Lookah is a manufacturer‑direct headshop rather than a reseller, which is why the range runs from a $20 replacement coil to a $300 e‑rig without a gap in the middle. The hardware side is theirs: the Seahorse line of electric nectar collectors, the 510 battery range, the Dragon Egg and Unicorn e‑rigs, and the enail kits. The glass side is theirs too, under Lookah Glass and the Tataoo and BIGMOM sub‑brands. The grinders, trays, torches and papers are curated third‑party stock they carry so a basket can be filled in one place, and they say so on their own site rather than passing it off.

This page is the whole catalogue as we last read it, grouped the way Lookah groups it. Prices and availability come from their own listings; we add nothing to them. Where a product links out, it goes to Lookah's page for that item.

What Lookah actually makes

The Seahorse is the product most people arrive for: an electric nectar collector that heats a quartz or ceramic tip on demand, so concentrate is taken straight from the dish without a torch. The line runs from the original Seahorse through the Pro, Pro Plus, Max and Queen, and the coils are consumable — which is why replacement coils are the most repeated item in their catalogue.

Their 510 battery range covers the other half of the same shopper: the Load, the Firebee and the Cat are slim variable‑voltage batteries for standard cartridges. Above those sit the e‑rigs — Dragon Egg, Unicorn, Octopus — which are self‑contained electric dab rigs with their own glass.

How the glass is organised

Lookah Glass covers bongs, dab rigs and the parts that fit them: bowls, ash catchers, bangers, carb caps and downstems. The bong range is sorted by shape and by novelty — beakers and straight tubes on one side, animal and figure pieces on the other — and the dab rigs split between glass and silicone. Their own site carries around 125 collections across this range; the six groups above are how they front them.
