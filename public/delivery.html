Delivery

Last updated 26 November 2026

The short version. We deliver orders ourselves, by hand, in Indiana. Most orders arrive in 1 to 3 business days. You pay in cash when it arrives, and the person who ordered must be there with a valid photo ID showing they are 21 or older.

Where we deliver

We hand-deliver to street addresses in Columbus and the surrounding area of South Central Indiana. If your address is farther out in Indiana, we'll tell you when we confirm your order whether we can reach it, and on what day. We do not deliver outside Indiana, and we do not ship by mail or carrier.

How long it takes

Items we have on hand are usually delivered within 1 to 3 business days of your order being confirmed. Some items are special ordered from our suppliers; those usually take 5 to 10 business days, and we tell you the expected date when we confirm. We'll contact you by phone or email to agree a delivery window.

What it costs

Local delivery is free in our regular area. If an address needs a delivery fee, we tell you before your order is confirmed, and nothing is added without your agreement. Prices include no hidden charges: you pay the item prices, any agreed delivery fee, and 7% Indiana sales tax.

The ID check at your door

Every order is for adults 21 and over. At delivery we check a valid, unexpired government photo ID (driver's license, state ID, passport or military ID). The name and photo must match the person who placed the order, and the date of birth must show they are 21 or older.

  • We will not hand an order to anyone under 21, to anyone without valid ID, or to anyone other than the person who ordered.
  • We never leave vaping, e-liquid, nicotine or tobacco products at a door, in a mailbox, or with a neighbor.
  • If we cannot complete the ID check, the order is not handed over. We'll try to rearrange once; after that the order is cancelled and nothing is charged.

Paying at delivery

We take cash only. We don't take credit or debit cards, online or at the door. You pay the order total when the order is handed over, and not before.

Questions

Email jake@legalleafmarket.shop with your order number and we'll answer within one business day.
