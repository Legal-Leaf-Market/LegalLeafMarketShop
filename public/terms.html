Terms

what this is,and what it is not

Written in plain English rather than in legal language, because a page nobody can read protects nobody. Last updated 31 October 2026.

I

the whole thing in one paragraph

A price list, with a small shop of our own

Legal-Leaf Market does two things, and it matters which one you are using.

Most of the site is a price list. We read the public product pages of other shops and work out what each listing actually costs per gram. For those listings we hold no stock, take no payment and set no prices. When you buy one, you buy from that shop, on their site, under their terms, and your contract is with them. If something goes wrong with that order, it is between you and the seller. We can sometimes help you find the right contact, and we have no power beyond that.

Some smoking accessories we sell ourselves. Those are marked Sold by Legal-Leaf Market and check out on our own store. For those, we are the seller, and chapter III sets out what that means. If an item does not carry that mark, we did not sell it.

II

how we get paid

Affiliate links, and our own sales, stated plainly

Most links out of this site carry a referral tag. If you buy after following one, the shop may pay us a commission. It costs you nothing extra, and any coupon shown is yours whether or not we earn anything. When you buy one of our own items, we earn the margin on it, the way any shop does.

What none of that money buys is placement. The default sort is cheapest per gram, and it is computed from the price. A shop cannot pay to appear higher, and our own items get no boost either. The ordering is arithmetic.

III

when you buy from us

Orders we sell and ship ourselves

This chapter applies only to items marked Sold by Legal-Leaf Market. The seller is Legal-Leaf Market LLC, an Indiana limited liability company.

Who can buy. You must be 21 or over and buying for yourself. Every order is age-verified at checkout, and age-restricted items need an adult signature with ID on delivery. We cancel any order we cannot verify.

What the products are for. Everything we sell is sold for use with tobacco and legal herbs. We do not sell flower, cannabis, nicotine or tobacco, and nothing we say about a product is a suggestion that you use it with anything unlawful where you are.

Where we ship. US addresses only. Some items cannot be sent to some states or cities; checkout refuses those, and if a rule changes after you order, we cancel and refund that item. Working out what is lawful where you live is still your responsibility.

Prices and orders. The price, shipping and any sales tax are shown at checkout before you pay. Your order is an offer to buy; it becomes a sale when we ship it and email you tracking. Until then we may cancel it, with a full refund, if the item is out of stock, the price was plainly a mistake, or we cannot lawfully send it.

Payment. Card payments are handled by our store platform and its payment processor. We never see or keep your full card number. If something is wrong with a charge, email us before going to your bank; we can usually fix it faster.

Shipping, breakage and returns are set out on their own pages, and those pages are part of these terms: Shipping and Returns. In short: broken on arrival is replaced or refunded, unused items can come back within 30 days, and anything that has been used cannot.

Warranty. Where a maker gives a warranty, it passes to you, and we will help you claim on it. Beyond that and what the Returns page promises, our items are sold as described on their product pages, with no further promise, to the extent the law allows. If we owe you anything for an order, it will not be more than you paid for it.

Which law. Indiana law governs sales we make, and any dispute that cannot be settled by email goes to the courts of Indiana. Nothing here takes away rights the law of your own state gives you as a consumer.

IV

the numbers

What the prices and lab figures mean, and do not

Other shops' prices are read from their pages and can be wrong. They may be stale, mid-change, or out of stock by the time you click. Shipping estimates for those shops are for comparison only and are not quotes. Always confirm the real price at checkout on the seller's site. If our number and theirs disagree, theirs is the real one. For our own items, the price at our checkout is the price.

Lab figures are read from the seller's own published certificates. Where a card shows a potency figure, we parsed a certificate for that specific product and checked that the report's own arithmetic reconciles. Where we could not, the card shows no figure rather than a guess.

The card also says what kind of document is behind it, a certificate for that product, a store-wide compliance sheet, or a link to the shop's lab-results page. Those are three different things and we do not merge them. The method is written up in What the Certificates Say.

We report what the lab said.
We do not vouch for the product.

We are not a laboratory and we do not test anything ourselves. A figure here is a faithful reading of a document the seller published, not our own verification of what is in the bag.

What we do not promise

Nothing here is a health claim. We make no statement that any product treats, prevents or cures anything, or that it is safe for you. Nothing on this site is medical advice. If you have a medical question, ask a doctor.

Nothing here is legal advice, and a product appearing on this site is not a statement that it is lawful where you are. What may be bought, sold, shipped or received varies by state and sometimes by city, and it changes. Working out what applies to you is your responsibility, and if the answer matters, ask a lawyer in your state. The background is set out in The Seam, which is history and explanation, not advice, and not a compliance check.

You must be 21 or over to use this site. Browsing asks you to confirm it on your first visit, and that is a confirmation, not a verification: we take your word for it. Buying from us is different, and is verified at checkout (chapter III). If you are under 21, this is not for you.

The price list is provided as it is. We do our best to keep it accurate and running, and we cannot promise it will be either. We are not liable for losses arising from relying on another shop's price, a lab figure, an availability flag or anything else we read from someone else's site, which is the honest position for a free comparison service, not a clause we are hiding behind.

If we change these terms, we will change this page and move the date at the top. An order is governed by the terms in place when you placed it.
