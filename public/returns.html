Returns and refunds

Last updated 26 November 2026

The short version. Unused items in their original packaging can come back within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Anything that arrives broken or doesn't work, we replace or refund. Email us first and we'll arrange a pickup.

Changing your mind

You can return an unused item in its original, unopened packaging within 30 days of delivery. Email jake@legalleafmarket.shop with your order number and what you'd like to return. We'll arrange to collect it, or you can hand it back at a later delivery. Once we have it, we refund the item price.

What can't come back

Anything that goes near somebody's mouth cannot be resold, so these are final once opened or used:

Final onceExamples
usedpipes, water pipes, vaporizers and their mouthpieces, chambers and coils
openede-liquid, pods, disposables, papers, cones, wraps, filter tips, screens, cleaning solutions
marked final saleclearance items, labeled on the product before you buy

None of this applies to an item that is broken or faulty. That is covered below.

Broken, faulty or wrong

If an item arrives damaged, doesn't work, or isn't what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of delivery, with a photo if you can. We'll replace it or refund it in full, your choice, and collect the original at no cost to you. A device that fails within the manufacturer's warranty period we will help you claim under that warranty.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid in cash, usually when we collect the item from you. Delivery fees, if any were charged, are refunded when the return is our mistake.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel any time before delivery at no cost. Email us or tell us when we call to confirm. Special-order items can be cancelled before they are ordered from our supplier; after that, we'll tell you if a cancellation is still possible.
