Returns and refunds

If something is not right

Last updated 25 November 2026.

Damaged, faulty or wrong items

Please check your order when we hand it over. If anything is broken, faulty or not what you ordered, tell us at the door or within 48 hours (jake@legalleafmarket.shop or call or text (812) 671-1780), with a photo if you can. We replace it or refund it in full, your choice.

Devices (vaporizers, batteries, e-rigs) that stop working within the maker’s warranty: we help you claim on the warranty, and where the maker allows it we swap the device ourselves.

Changed your mind

Unused, unopened items in their original packaging can come back within 14 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Contact us and we arrange to collect it in Columbus, free of charge.

What cannot be returned

  • Anything opened or used, including glass that has been used, for hygiene and safety reasons.
  • Vapes, disposables, pods, e-liquids, nicotine pouches, cigars and tobacco once the seal or packaging is opened.
  • Herbal blends and teas once opened.

This does not affect items that arrive damaged, faulty or wrong; those are always covered above.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel at any time before delivery for a full refund. For a special-order item we have already ordered for you, we refund it in full unless the supplier charges us a restocking fee, which we will have told you about before you ordered.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, within 5 business days of us agreeing the refund or collecting the item. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. We do not refund in cash. If something looks wrong with a charge, please contact us before your bank; we can usually fix it the same day.
