Returns

sending somethingback

This covers orders you place with Legal-Leaf Market directly. If you bought from another shop after following one of our links, that shop's returns policy applies. Last updated 31 October 2026.

I

the short version

30 days, unused, in the original packaging

If you change your mind, you can return an unused item in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Email us first; please do not post anything back without a return number.

II

what cannot come back

Used items are final, and here is why

Anything that goes near somebody's mouth cannot be resold to somebody else, so once it has been used it cannot be returned. That means:

not returnable onceexamples
used or smoked throughpipes, bongs, rigs, bangers, mouthpieces, vaporizer mouthpieces and chambers
openedpapers, cones, wraps, filter tips, screens, cleaning solutions
marked final saleclearance items, said so on the product page before you buy

None of this applies to an item that arrived broken or does not work. That is covered below, and you are never out of pocket for it.

III

how

How to send it back

  1. Email hello@legal-leafmarket.com with your order number and what you are returning.
  2. We reply with a return number and the address to send it to.
  3. Pack it well (glass especially) and send it with tracking. Return postage is yours to pay unless we got the order wrong.
  4. When it arrives and we have checked it, we refund you.
IV

our mistakes

Broken, faulty or the wrong thing

If an item arrives broken, see Shipping: email us within 48 hours with photos and we replace or refund it, no return needed.

If we sent the wrong item, or something does not work when you first use it, tell us within 30 days. We pay the return postage and send a replacement or a full refund, including what you paid for shipping.

Many vaporizers and electronic grinders also carry the manufacturer's warranty, which runs longer than 30 days. After our 30 days are up, we will help you make a warranty claim with the maker.

V

the money

Refunds

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, within 5 business days of the return reaching us. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. On a change-of-mind return, the original shipping charge is not refunded.

If you would rather swap an item for a different one, say so in your email and we will arrange an exchange instead.
