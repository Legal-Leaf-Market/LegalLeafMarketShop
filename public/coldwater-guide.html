What a gram actually costs in Coldwater

Six dispensaries sit within about a mile of I‑69 exit 13, and more than half the people walking into them drove up from Indiana. Every one of those menus prices by the package — an eighth here, an ounce there — and not one of them tells you the price per gram, which is the only number that compares them. So here is the tool, free, no signup, nothing to install.

The benchmark

Start from what Michigan actually averages

The state's Cannabis Regulatory Agency publishes an average adult-use retail flower price every month. It is the fairest yardstick there is, because it is computed from real transactions across the whole state rather than from anybody's advertising.

$58.18 average retail ounce of adult-use flower, statewide June 2026
$2.08 the same figure expressed per gram — the line everything below is measured against Derived
$20 median price of an eighth in Michigan, against $35 in Ohio Mid-2026

Michigan has the cheapest flower in the country, and that is the entire reason the drive happens. It is also why the per-gram question matters more here than anywhere else: when the floor is this low, the gap between a good buy and a bad one is enormous in percentage terms and nearly invisible in dollars.

    The one thing worth internalising

    A $20 eighth is $5.71 a gram. A $58 ounce is $2.08 a gram. The eighth feels like the cheap option because it is a smaller number, and it costs nearly three times as much for the same weight. That single comparison is why this page exists, and no menu on the Green Mile will make it for you.

    Before you drive back

    The part nobody at the counter is going to explain

    Indiana has no legal adult-use market, and carrying cannabis across the state line is a federal and Indiana offence regardless of where you bought it. Buying it legally in Michigan does not travel with it. Branch and Steuben county sheriffs have both said publicly that a large share of their cannabis arrests are people heading south after a Michigan purchase — very often stopped for something minor first, with the smell doing the rest.

    That is not a scare story and it is not legal advice. It is the single most predictable way a cheap ounce turns expensive, and it is worth knowing before you plan the trip rather than after.

    If you're coming from Ohio instead

    Same rule at that line. The price gap is real — Ohio's median eighth has been running about $35 against Michigan's $20 — but the drive back is the risk, not the purchase.

    Notes on method

    1. The statewide average retail price of an ounce of adult-use flower is published monthly by the Michigan Cannabis Regulatory Agency. The $58.18 figure is the June 2026 print; April was $59.05 and May $59.73. The per-gram figure of $2.08 is that number divided by 28.
    2. Median eighth prices for Michigan and Ohio are drawn from mid-2026 trade reporting, not from our own sampling, and they are medians across each state rather than quotes from any particular shop.
    3. The dispensary list was assembled from public business listings in August 2026 and is offered as a starting point, not a register. Shops open, close and move; one entry here carries an unconfirmed address and is marked as such. We publish no prices for any of them.
    4. The comparison tool runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is transmitted, logged or stored.
    5. Conversions use net product weight. A package's price per gram says nothing about its quality, its potency, or whether it is whole flower rather than shake — a very cheap gram is often cheap for a reason worth asking about at the counter.
