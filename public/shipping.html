Delivery policy

How your order gets to you

Last updated 25 November 2026.

The short version

We hand-deliver orders ourselves in and around Columbus, Indiana. Delivery is free. We do not ship by mail or courier, and we do not deliver outside Indiana.

Every delivery is to an adult 21 or older, and we check a valid, unexpired government photo ID at the door, for every order, whatever is in it.

When it arrives

Some items are in stock locally and can usually be delivered within one to two days. Most of the catalogue is special order: we order it from the maker or distributor after you order, and deliver it when it arrives, usually within three to seven days. We text you when your order is ready and agree a delivery window with you.

If an item turns out to be unavailable, we tell you and you choose: wait, swap it, or a full refund for that item.

Where we deliver

Columbus, Indiana and nearby. If you are further out in Indiana, contact us first and we will tell you whether we can reach you. Orders with a delivery address outside Indiana are refused at checkout.

The ID check

At delivery we check that the person receiving the order is the buyer named on it, or another adult at the same address, and is 21 or older, using a valid photo ID (driver’s license, state ID, passport or military ID).

If nobody 21 or older with valid ID is there, we do not hand the order over. We will try once more at a time you choose. If the order cannot be handed over, we refund it, less any restocking cost a special-order supplier charges us, which we tell you before you order.

We will not deliver to anyone who appears to be buying for someone under 21.

Packaging

Orders are handed over in plain packaging with nothing on the outside about what is inside.

Sales tax and contact

Indiana sales tax (7%) is added at checkout. Questions about an order: jake@legalleafmarket.shop or call or text (812) 671-1780.
