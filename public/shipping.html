Shipping

how your ordergets to you

This covers orders you place with Legal-Leaf Market directly. If you followed a link to another shop and bought there, that shop's shipping policy applies, not this one. Last updated 31 October 2026.

I

the short version

Packed in Indiana, out in one to two business days

Orders usually leave within one to two business days of being placed. You get a tracking number by email as soon as the label is printed. Shipping cost is shown at checkout before you pay, and nothing is added afterwards.

II

where

Where we ship, and where we cannot

We ship to US street addresses, and to PO boxes for items that go by USPS. Items that need a signature cannot go to a PO box. We do not ship outside the US.

Some products cannot go to some places. Rules on smoking accessories, vaporizers and hemp products differ by state and sometimes by city, and they change. If an item cannot be shipped to your address, checkout will say so and will not take the order. If a rule changes after you order and we can no longer lawfully send something, we cancel that item and refund it in full.

III

how

Carriers, signatures and age checks

whathow it ships
Glass, pipes, grinders, papers, storageUSPS, UPS or FedEx, whichever is quickest to you
Vaporizers and other age-restricted itemsa carrier that accepts them, adult (21+) signature required on delivery

Every order is age-verified at checkout. Where a signature is required, the carrier checks ID at the door and will not leave the parcel. If nobody 21 or over is there, the carrier will try again or hold it for collection.

Packages are sent in plain outer packaging. The label shows "Legal-Leaf Market" and nothing about what is inside.

IV

glass breaks

If it arrives broken

Glass is packed to travel, but carriers drop things. If something arrives broken or damaged, email us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos of the item and the box it came in. Keep the packaging until we reply; the carrier sometimes asks to see it.

We will send a replacement or refund it in full, your choice, and you will not be asked to send the broken pieces back.

V

when it does not turn up

Late, lost or marked delivered

If tracking has not moved for 7 days, or it says delivered and you do not have it, email us. We open a claim with the carrier, and if the package cannot be found we replace it or refund it. Please check with neighbours and your mailroom first; most "missing" parcels turn up within a day.

If an order comes back to us because the address was wrong or a signature delivery was never collected, we refund the items once it arrives back, less the original shipping cost.

VI

tax and contact

Sales tax, and how to reach us

Sales tax is added at checkout where the law requires it: Indiana's 7% on orders delivered in Indiana, and other states' rates where we are registered to collect.

Anything about an order goes to hello@legal-leafmarket.com. Put your order number in the subject line and we will usually reply within one business day.

See also Returns, Terms and Privacy. Adults 21+ only.
