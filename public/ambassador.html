Bring your town onto Legal-Leaf

We compare every dispensary in a town by price per gram, side by side, and show which shop is actually cheapest for the thing you were going to buy anyway. Coldwater, Michigan is the first one.

What an ambassador actually does

This is a real job, not a badge, and it is worth being straight about it before you apply. A town stays on this site because somebody local keeps it fresh.

  • Run the menu collector once or twice a week. It is a bookmarklet: you open each shop’s own menu in your own browser, tap it, and it reads what is on the page and sends it over. A full town takes about ten minutes.
  • Tell us when a shop opens, closes, moves, or changes its menu system.
  • Flag prices that look wrong. You know the shops; we only know their HTML.

We meet first, properly — a call or in person. Partly so you can ask what you are signing up for, and partly because putting a town live and letting it go stale is worse than never launching it at all.

What we are not asking for

  • No money, ever. It costs you nothing to bring your town on.
  • No relationship with the shops. We are not affiliated with any dispensary or brand, we take nothing from them, and no shop can pay to rank higher. You are not selling anything to anybody.
