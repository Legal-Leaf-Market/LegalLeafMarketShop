Coldwater collector

Reads the dispensary menu you are looking at and sends it to /api/coldwater. It automates “copy what is on my screen” — it does not go anywhere your browser was not already taken.

1 · Install

Drag this to your bookmarks bar:

Grab menu

On a phone, or if you cannot drag: use the console method below. It works everywhere.

Blocked on Dutchie? Drag this one instead:

Grab menu (self-contained)

Building…

The one above loads the collector from this site when you click it, and a shop with a strict script-src refuses that — Dutchie does, which is why nothing happens there. This one carries the whole collector inside the bookmark, so there is nothing to load and nothing to block.

This bookmark is a snapshot. It carries the collector as it is today and never updates itself. Current build: … — the collector’s panel prints its own build on the shop page. If the two differ, re-drag this button. A stale reader does not fail; it returns a smaller menu that looks fine.

2 · Capture

  1. Open a shop’s menu from the table below and let it finish loading — scroll to the bottom so lazy-loaded rows are really on the page.
  2. Click the Grab menu bookmark.
  3. Check the count in the panel, confirm the store key, paste your admin token, press Send.
  4. Come back here and press Refresh to confirm it landed.
Dutchie menus sit in a cross-origin iframe. A bookmarklet on the shop’s own page cannot read inside one, and that is the same-origin policy doing its job rather than a bug to work around. Right-click the menu → This Frame → Open Frame in New Tab, then run the bookmarklet there.

Console method

Open the menu, press F12 → Console, paste, Enter. Some sites block pasting until you type allow pasting first.

This copies the whole script rather than a one-line loader on purpose: a loader still has to fetch from another origin, which is exactly what a strict CSP forbids. Pasted source has nothing left to block.
