What this is not

It is not an offer to sell. We hold no stock, take no payment, set no prices and are not a party to any transaction. Every link goes to the seller's own product page and every term of sale is theirs.

It is not a compliance check. Whether a given lot may lawfully be bought, sold, shipped or received depends on your state and sometimes your city, on licensing, and on rules that differ on whether they measure delta-9 or total THC. A lot appearing here is never a statement that it is legal for you. That is the subject of The Seam, and a per-state view of it is the thing we would need to build next and will not publish without a lawyer.

The lab column is deliberately three-valued. Measured means we parsed a certificate for that specific product and its own arithmetic checks out. Document means a certificate exists but is a store-level sheet or an index page rather than a test of that lot. None means no report we could read. We do not merge the second into the first, and at these order sizes that distinction is the entire point. The method is written up in What the Certificates Say.

And prices move. These are scraped from public pages and can be stale, wrong, or out of stock by the time you click. Confirm everything with the seller.
